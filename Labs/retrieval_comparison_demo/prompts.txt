SUNNY CAFE: SIMPLE CLASSROOM PROMPTS
===================================
Read the 8 tiny files in data/ or expand the dataset panel in Gradio.
Use 1 passage and turn answer generation OFF at first.

1. MATCH WORDS
What is the price of Mango Milkshake?
Correct note: 01-mango-milkshake. Price: Rs90.
Explain: the question repeats the menu name. All methods may succeed.

2. MATCH MEANING
Can you bring my order to my house?
Correct note: 04-home-delivery.
Explain: the note uses purchases/address, not order/house. Takeaway is different:
the customer collects it at the counter. Look at the actual ranking in each mode.
Correct delivery conditions: Rs200 minimum bill, Rs20 fee, 11am-7pm Mon-Sat.

3. FIND THE EXCEPTION
My mango milkshake has gone sour. Can I get a refund after opening it?
Correct note: 07-spoiled-drink.
Explain: 01 describes the menu; 06 gives the general change-of-mind rule; 07 is
the spoiled-product exception. An opened spoiled drink can be replaced or
refunded within 24 hours with a receipt. Inspect before/after reranking.

4. CHECK THE OPPOSITE CONDITION
I opened a lemonade and simply changed my mind. Can I get a refund?
Correct note: 06-change-of-mind. Answer: no, not for a simple change of mind.
Explain: the spoiled-drink exception does not apply to this question.

5. EASY CONTROL QUESTION
What time does the cafe close on Saturday?
Correct note: 08-opening-hours. Answer: 8pm.
Explain: all three methods may give the same good result.

6. NO ANSWER IN THE DATA
What is the cafe's Wi-Fi password?
No correct source exists. The answer should say the evidence does not contain
it. Vector and hybrid may return nearby notes; retrieval is not proof of an answer.

TEACHING STEPS
1. Read all notes. Everyone can understand the complete data in a minute.
2. Ask question 1 with Keyword mode.
3. Ask question 2; switch Keyword -> Vector -> Hybrid + rerank without editing it.
4. Ask question 3. Compare the menu, general rule and exception passages.
5. Turn answers ON. Compare citations with the selected note.
6. Increase passage count from 1 to 3 and observe whether differences shrink.
7. Finish with question 6 to demonstrate abstention.

No fake rankings or scripted answers are used. Rankings can vary by embedding
model and Search service revision. Compare methods using the SAME evidence count.
