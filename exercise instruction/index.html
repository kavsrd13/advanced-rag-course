From Transformer models to RAG and agents

Follow the four stages in order. Each card opens the complete exercise. Course numbers below show the teaching sequence; original lab numbers and Microsoft exercise numbers remain in their source pages.

1 · Hugging Face 2 · Foundry and GenAI apps 3 · RAG patterns 4 · Agents
Prepare environments and resources The Hugging Face exercises use Google Colab. If later exercises reuse an Azure resource group, keep it until those exercises are complete, then follow the cleanup steps for resources you own.
